# Stall Performance Analysis: Wanjiku, Gikomba Market

**Author:** Alice Mungai
**Cohort:** 1 Million Devs Cohort

## Business problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh produce stall in Gikomba Market. She records daily sales and stock by hand and cannot easily tell which days earn the most, whether the business is growing, or which products are running low. 
This project uses core Python to summarise 14 days of sales, classify days as Strong, Normal or Weak,
flag items that need reordering, and recommend actions.

## How to run
[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Alice34mung3ai/-plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

Click the link and choose **Runtime → Run all**. No installs needed.

## Top findings
1. Sales grew **+10.66%** week on week (KES 22,050 → KES 24,400).
2. Friday to Sunday brought in **58.3%** of all sales (KES 27,100 of KES 46,450).
3. **2 of 5** products are below reorder level: Tomatoes (18 kg vs 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg).
